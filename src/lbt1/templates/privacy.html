Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-06-01 · Locksmith Daddy, a Cetabo LLC venture

What we collect

  • Account info: email, name, phone, and (for some plans) business license number.
  • VIN lookups: the VINs you submit, the part numbers we return, the dealer URLs that confirmed each match, the date of each lookup, and how long it took. Stored under your account so you can review past results.
  • Payment info: processed entirely by Stripe. We never see or store full card numbers — only the card brand and last 4 digits returned by Stripe.
  • Technical signals: IP address, browser user-agent, and a non-identifying browser fingerprint. Used only for anti-abuse (preventing trial-account farming).

What we do with it

  • Run your VIN lookup and return the OEM part number.
  • Store your lookup history so you can view past results.
  • Send you transactional emails (signup confirmation, lookup results, payment receipts). You can opt out of newsletter / promotional emails at signup or any time after.
  • Detect and block abuse (rate limits, duplicate accounts, scrapers).

We do not sell or rent your data. We do not share your VIN or lookup history with anyone outside Cetabo LLC.

Third parties we use

  • Stripe — payment processing. Their privacy policy.
  • ScrapFly — anti-bot proxy we route dealer scrapes through. They see the VIN and the dealer URL we fetched. They do not see your account info.
  • NHTSA VPIC API — public US Department of Transportation VIN-decode service. They see the VIN being decoded.
  • Fly.io — our hosting provider.
  • Dealer parts websites (Toyota, Hyundai, Kia, Genesis, etc.) — we visit their public catalogs to retrieve the OEM part number. They see ScrapFly's IP, not yours.

Cookies

We set one HTTP-only session cookie when you sign in, valid for 30 days. We do not use third-party tracking, advertising, or analytics cookies.

Lookup retention

Lookup records are stored under your account so you can review past results. You can delete any lookup from your dashboard. Account deletion removes all associated lookups.

Your rights

You can request a copy of your data, correction of inaccurate data, or full deletion of your account at any time by emailing cetabo.contact@gmail.com. We honor GDPR / CCPA requests within 30 days.

Children

Locksmith Daddy is a B2B tool for professional locksmiths. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we materially change this policy, we will email you at least 14 days in advance. The latest version is always at /privacy on this site.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email cetabo.contact@gmail.com.
