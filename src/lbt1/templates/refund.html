Refund Policy

Effective 2026-06-01 · Locksmith Daddy, a Cetabo LLC venture
Zero-refund policy. All sales are final.

Why we can do this honestly

You only get charged for a credit when we return a dealer-verified OEM part number. If the tool runs but cannot locate a verified PN for your VIN, the credit is not consumed and your balance is unchanged. You will never pay for a "no result."

This is enforced server-side at the moment the result is generated — not in retrospect after a customer service ticket. The check is automatic; there's nothing to dispute.

What "verified" means here

The tool returns a PN only when the dealer's own catalog page explicitly attests that the PN fits your VIN's year, make, and model. Every result includes a link to that dealer page as evidence — you can click through and verify the attestation yourself before cutting.

This is a stricter bar than "looks close," "same family," or "probably fits." If the dealer hasn't attested fitment, we say "not yet attested" and you are not charged a credit.

Exceptions to the zero-refund policy

The only situation where we will issue a refund is a duplicate charge caused by a technical error on our side (Stripe processed your card twice for the same lookup credit). Email contact@locksmithdaddy.us with the Stripe receipt IDs and we'll reverse the duplicate within 5 business days.

Chargebacks

Initiating a chargeback for a successfully delivered VIN lookup (one where a dealer-verified PN was returned) will result in your account being permanently closed. This policy exists to protect the price for honest customers — every chargeback fee we absorb is a price we'd otherwise pass on.

Subscription / enterprise plans

Monthly subscription plans are billed in advance and are non-refundable for the current billing period. You can cancel at any time; cancellation stops future billing but does not refund the current month. Unused credits in your account do not expire and remain available after subscription cancellation.

Acceptance

By submitting payment on Locksmith Daddy, you acknowledge and agree to this refund policy.
